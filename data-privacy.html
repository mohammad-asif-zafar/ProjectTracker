Data Privacy Policy

ProTrack – Project and Task Tracker

Effective date: To be confirmed
Application: ProTrack – Project and Task Tracker

1. Introduction

ProTrack is a project and task management application designed to help users organize projects, tasks and progress.

We value your privacy and aim to minimize the collection and processing of personal information.

2. Information We Collect

ProTrack is designed to operate without requiring an account or registration.

Project names, descriptions, tasks, subtasks, activity logs, and application preferences are stored locally on your device.

The application is not designed to automatically upload this information to external developer servers.

3. How Information Is Used

Information entered into ProTrack is used to provide application features, including:

  • Project management.
  • Task and subtask management.
  • Progress calculations.
  • Reports and summaries.
  • Application preferences.

4. Data Storage & Android Backup

Application data is stored locally on your device using local SQLite database storage (SQLDelight).

Because `android:allowBackup="true"` is enabled in the Android Manifest, your local app database and preferences may be included in encrypted device backups (such as Google Drive backup) or transferred to a new device during device-to-device setup, as managed by the Android system backup framework.

5. Data Sharing

ProTrack works without cloud synchronization, advertising or analytics.

Project information is not intentionally transmitted to third-party servers by the application's core functionality.

6. Notifications

If notification functionality is enabled, the application may use device notification capabilities to provide relevant project or task reminders.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Your locally stored information remains on your device until you delete it through the application or remove the application's stored data:

  • Deleting a project automatically cascades to delete all associated subtasks and activity logs.
  • Uninstalling the app removes local database files, though previously backed-up data in personal cloud backup (if enabled on the device) may persist until overwritten or deleted via your device backup settings.

8. Children's Privacy

ProTrack is a general-purpose productivity application. It is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when application functionality or data practices change.

The latest version will be published on the official ProTrack website.
