ProTrack Privacy Policy

ProTrack – Project and Task Tracker

Effective Date: To be confirmed
Last Updated: November 2026
Application: ProTrack – Project and Task Tracker

1. Introduction and Application Identification

Welcome to ProTrack ("we", "our", or "us"). ProTrack is an offline-first project and task management mobile application built for Android and iOS. This Privacy Policy explains how ProTrack handles your information when you use our application.

2. Information Accessed and Collected

ProTrack is designed with privacy at its core. The application does not require user registration, login, or account creation.

We do not collect, transmit, or harvest personal information, device identifiers, location data, or telemetry. All project names, task descriptions, subtask completion metrics, and user preferences remain strictly on your local device.

3. Information Stored Locally on the Device

All data entered into ProTrack is stored locally on your device utilizing secure SQLite storage via SQLDelight. This includes:

  • Project titles, short names, keywords, icons, and descriptions.
  • Subtask items, progress states, notes, and timestamps.
  • Local application settings (theme preferences and notification toggles).

This data is stored solely in your device's local sandbox storage and is never uploaded to remote servers.

4. How Information Is Used

Locally stored information is used exclusively to provide application functionality, including:

  • Project organization and hierarchy management.
  • Task tracking and real-time progress calculation.
  • Local history logging and summary reports.
  • Applying user theme and notification preferences.

5. Information Sharing and Third-Party Services

ProTrack does not share, sell, rent, or trade any user information with third parties. We do not integrate advertising networks, analytics trackers, or third-party SDKs that collect user data.

6. Network Communications and External Services

ProTrack operates entirely offline. The application makes zero outbound network requests or remote API calls. No data is transmitted outside your device.

7. Permissions and Their Purposes

ProTrack requests minimal device permissions necessary for core operation. No sensitive runtime permissions (such as camera, microphone, location, or contacts) are required.

8. Data Security and Protection

Because all information is stored locally within your device's secure application sandbox, security is governed by your device's built-in operating system protections and lock screen security.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Your data remains on your device until you delete it within the application or uninstall ProTrack. Uninstalling the application will remove its local database and associated stored data. You can also clear application storage at any time via your device's system settings.

10. Children's Privacy

ProTrack is a general-purpose productivity tool and does not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13.

11. Changes to the Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any revisions will be published on this official webpage with a revised effective date.
